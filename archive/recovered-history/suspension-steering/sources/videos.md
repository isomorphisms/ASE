## Source discipline

- Tire aspect ratio is sidewall section height as a percentage of section width.
- Recommended inflation pressure comes from the vehicle manufacturer's placard/service information, not the tire sidewall maximum.
- Ball-joint inspection loading and direction are suspension-design specific.
- SAI/included-angle interpretation depends on signed-angle convention and the actual suspension geometry.
