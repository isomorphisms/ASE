# Suspension and steering source channels

## Driver's Therapy

Broad ASE review, practical repair framing, and diagnostics. Principal instructor David Rosckowff publicly identifies as an ASE Master Technician.

## McCuistian

Richard McCuistian's instructional material is useful for question-driven diagnosis and for distinguishing symptoms from causes. Public professional biographies identify him as an ASE Certified Master Automobile Technician with decades of technician, instructor, and technical-writing experience.

## Hunter Learning Channel

Corporate alignment-training source. Especially useful for measurement vocabulary and diagnostic use of camber, caster, toe, SAI, included angle, and related geometry.

## OldSchoolNoe

Recovered study history contains a coherent suspension/steering series covering alignment angles, tires, steering linkage, bump steer, memory steer, front-suspension components, and angle changes. Treat it as a useful teaching source; do not elevate a test-prep shortcut into a universal vehicle rule without cross-checking.

## Master Automotive Training

Automotive technical school publishing broad ASE review and diagnostic material.

## Auto Pro Teacher

Recovered study history contains the channel's tire-size lesson. Use for notation, cross-checked against tire-industry/OEM references.

## 1A Auto / Donut / Fast Monty's Garage / Suspensions Explained

Supplementary visual and practical explanations. Useful for alternate views of tire service, alignment measurement, and torque steer; stronger OEM/measurement sources should control vehicle-specific procedures.
