# Purge and vent valves

The playlist has several valve-focused videos. The points below are a general A8 study frame, not transcript-derived claims.

## Purge valve

The purge valve meters the path from the canister toward engine vacuum. A useful bench or on-car check asks more than whether the solenoid clicks:

- does it seal when it should be closed?
- does it flow when commanded open?
- does electrical resistance/current make sense?
- does commanded purge produce a plausible pressure/vacuum or fuel-trim response?

A purge valve stuck open can behave like an unintended vapor/vacuum path. A valve stuck closed prevents commanded purge flow. Electrical faults and mechanical leakage are different failure modes.

## Vent valve

The vent valve controls the atmospheric side of the EVAP system. During leak testing the system may need that path sealed; during normal operation it must also be able to breathe as designed.

Separate:

- valve mechanically stuck open;
- valve mechanically stuck closed or path blocked;
- solenoid/wiring/power/driver fault;
- contamination at the vent/filter;
- system leak elsewhere that merely produces a similar monitor failure.
