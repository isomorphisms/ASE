# Source channels and instructors

The point of this file is to record **why a source deserves attention**, not to create a celebrity ranking. Credentials are included only when publicly stated and independently findable.

## Driver's Therapy

Driver's Therapy says its library contains more than 1,000 videos on diagnostics, repair, modification, ASE preparation, electrical testing, and automotive careers. Its principal instructor, **David Rosckowff**, publicly identifies as an ASE Master Technician; his biography also describes SCCA ITA racing and HPDE instruction experience.

Use for: broad ASE review, practical repair framing, diagnostics.

Sources:
- https://driverstherapy.com/
- https://driverstherapy.com/about/

## McCuistian

The channel is the instructional channel of **Richard McCuistian**. A professional biography describes nearly 50 years in the automotive field as a technician, instructor, and technical writer, and identifies him as an ASE Certified Master Automobile Technician. The same biography says he has developed training material and maintains a large instructional YouTube library.

Use for: question-driven diagnosis and the habit of distinguishing symptoms from causes.

Source:
- https://blog.carparts.com/blog/our-team/

## Master Automotive Training

Master Automotive Training is a technical school in Rancho Cucamonga, California. Founder/lead instructor **Oscar Gomez** is publicly described by the school as a California-certified instructor with more than 15 years of industry/instructional experience and 18+ ASE certifications. The school emphasizes live-vehicle diagnostics and instrument use rather than answer memorization.

Use for: broad ASE review and diagnostic procedure.

Sources:
- https://masterautomotivetraining.com/pages/who-we-are
- https://autodiagnostic.masterautomotivetraining.com/

## Hunter Learning Channel

Hunter Learning Channel is a corporate training source rather than one individual's channel. Hunter Engineering says it has more than 40 years of industry training experience, that its classes are led by industry-certified instructors, and that training material goes through a review process. Its alignment material is especially useful because Hunter manufactures and teaches the measurement equipment that exposes camber, caster, toe, SAI, included angle, and related geometry.

Use for: alignment measurement vocabulary, measurement procedure, and diagnostic use of otherwise non-adjustable angles.

Source:
- https://www.hunter.com/training/

## OldSchoolNoe

Recovered study history shows a coherent A4 series covering alignment angles, tires, steering linkage, bump steer, memory steer, suspension components, and angle changes. Public video descriptions confirm the #3 and #4 subject matter.

I have **not** independently verified a professional biography or credential claim for the creator in this pass. Treat this as a useful teaching source, not as an authority merely because a rule appears in a test-prep video.

Use for: compact A4 conceptual organization and exam-oriented diagnostic distinctions.

## Auto Pro Teacher

Recovered study history contains the channel's A4 tire-size lesson. No independently verified instructor biography has been added yet.

Use for: tire-size notation, cross-checked against tire-industry/OEM references.

## 1A Auto

Commercial repair-parts/DIY publisher. Its long tire overview is useful as a visual survey of size markings, date codes, pressure, rotation, wear, and service considerations. Treat repair recommendations as practical-source claims and cross-check safety-critical procedures against tire-industry/OEM guidance.

## Donut / Fast Monty's Garage / Suspensions Explained

These are supplementary explanatory sources recovered around the same study period. They are valuable for visualization and alternate explanations, but the current ASE task list and OEM/Hunter-style measurement procedures remain the stronger spine for exam scope and vehicle-specific service.
