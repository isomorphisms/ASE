## Source discipline

A useful video can still contain a shortcut, an oversimplification, or advice that is correct only for one suspension/brake design. When a video rule conflicts with the current ASE task list, an OEM service procedure, or a stronger technical source, preserve the disagreement instead of flattening it.

In particular:

- Tire **aspect ratio** is sidewall section height as a percentage of section width; it is not simply a tire "depth" measurement.
- Recommended inflation pressure comes from the vehicle manufacturer's placard/service information for the application, not from treating the tire sidewall's maximum pressure as the target.
- Ball-joint inspection loading and direction are suspension-design specific.
- SAI/included-angle interpretation depends on signed-angle convention and the actual suspension geometry.
