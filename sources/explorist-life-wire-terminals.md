## Why this belongs in mechatronics

The source is aimed at mobile/marine/off-grid electrical systems, but the workmanship topics transfer directly to machine wiring:

- cutting without deforming conductors;
- stripping insulation without nicking strands;
- matching crimp terminal and conductor size;
- using the correct crimp profile and tool;
- lug and ring-terminal preparation for higher-current connections;
- ferrules for stranded conductors entering suitable screw or spring terminals;
- environmental sealing and strain relief;
- splice quality;
- conductor routing and mechanical support;
- inspecting a finished termination rather than trusting that a crimp merely “looks tight.”

The lug and ring-terminal lessons are especially relevant to battery, DC-bus, starter, inverter, and other high-current terminations.
