## Study frame

The sensor videos fit a useful common pattern: identify the physical quantity being measured, understand the expected electrical signal, verify power/ground/reference where applicable, compare live or measured output against operating conditions, and only then decide whether the sensor itself is bad.

The ECU/CAN videos are kept here because they are closely related to interpreting missing or implausible sensor data, even though they are control/communication topics rather than sensors themselves.
